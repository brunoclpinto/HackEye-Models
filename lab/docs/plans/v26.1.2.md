User request: "testing using chrome on ios, image area does not accept pinch zoom nor scroll, it should. when i click an
item in the image need bounding box with class name like its used in hackeye lab."

## 1. Inspect tool and the selection box
- **New tool `V` Inspect** (button "Inspect", first in the Tools group). It is the **default tool** when the page opens,
  so a click or tap never paints by accident. Painting starts only after you pick Brush, Erase and the rest.
- **What a click or tap selects:** the **item** under the pointer, which is the 4-connected region of that pixel's class
  (the same flood fill the Region tool uses, without changing anything). The selection stores the class id, the pixel
  count, the bbox and a region bitmap.
- **Drawing**, in the style of the HackEye Lab (`~/repos/HackEye/RnD/lab/static/js/overlay.js`, `drawHazard`):
  - a box stroked 3 px in screen space in the class colour, with a thin dark outer stroke so it reads on any background;
  - the region tinted at about 15% in the class colour;
  - a label tag filled with the class colour, placed above the box's top-left corner (inside the box when there's no room
    above), clamped to the canvas;
  - label text `<class name> · id <id> · <N> px`, with ` · <merged name>` appended when view-through-map is on;
  - text dark or light, chosen from the colour's luminance;
  - the box and label stay sharp at any zoom, because they are drawn in screen space.
- **Clearing:** `Esc`, changing frame, or any edit that changes pixels of the selected region (it's re-run on the next
  click).
- **Readout:** the status bar shows the selection.
- **Shortcut:** with a selection, `Enter` relabels the selected region to the active class, respecting protect, as one
  undoable patch. Everywhere else, the other tools keep their click behaviour.
- **On every tab:** in Taxonomy and Rules the image is always in Inspect mode (no painting there).

## 2. Touch (iOS Chrome and Safari are both WebKit; also Android Chrome)
- **Two fingers, any tool:** pinch zooms around the midpoint between the fingers (the same range as the wheel, fit to 32×),
  and moving both fingers pans. Page zoom on the image is suppressed: `touch-action` on the canvas, plus
  `preventDefault` on WebKit's `gesturestart`/`gesturechange`.
- **One finger, Inspect tool:**
  - **at fit zoom**, vertical drags scroll the page natively (`touch-action: pan-y` while at fit), and a tap selects;
  - **zoomed in**, a drag pans the image and a tap selects.
- **One finger, editing tools:** the tool works as with a mouse (paint, lasso, SAM point), and the page doesn't scroll.
  The lasso is freehand drag only. SAM needs negative points, which a touch can't give with a right-click, so a "−" toggle
  button in the Tools group makes the next taps negative.
- **Double-tap** toggles fit and 4× at the tap point.
- **Pointer events throughout** (`pointerType` touch, pen or mouse). Tool actions are cancelled cleanly when a second
  finger lands; a stroke already started is rolled back.
- **Layout at phone width (390–430 px):**
  - the filter bar wraps, and the nav row stays on one line;
  - the work panel's sub-groups stack in one column;
  - tap targets are at least 36 px;
  - the page doesn't scroll sideways, and the image fits the width.
  - Check that `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">` is present.

## 3. Tests
- **E2E (Chromium, desktop):**
  - Inspect is the default tool;
  - a click shows a selection whose `__lab.state().selection` has the right class id and bbox, checked against
    `getPixel` and a flood fill on the fixture mask;
  - `Esc` clears it;
  - `Enter` relabels the region (the pixels on disk change after a save; undo restores them).
- **E2E (touch):** a Playwright context with `hasTouch` at an iPhone-sized viewport, using Chromium touch emulation and
  WebKit if the image has it.
  - synthetic two-pointer pinch through dispatched `PointerEvent`s changes the zoom and keeps the midpoint pixel fixed
    within 1 px;
  - one-finger drag pans when zoomed;
  - a tap selects;
  - the page has no horizontal scroll at 428 px wide.
- **Test ids:** `tool-inspect`, `sam-negative`, `selection-label` (text). Add `selection` to the `window.__lab.state()`
  hook.
- Existing tests that click the canvas must pick their tool explicitly; adapt them where they relied on Brush being the
  default.
- Unit tests are unchanged.
